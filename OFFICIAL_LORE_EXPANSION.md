# Official faction lore expansion — 2026-10-29

## Scope

All 17 verified N26 factions keep their six introductory chapters and gain 92 individually cited chapters: 194 topics total, 10–13 per faction. Korean body text grows from approximately 800–950 characters to 2,038–2,971 per faction. The registry contains 82 distinct official public URLs whose text was fetched and checked. English and Korean summaries are independently written, not reproduced source text.

Every new chapter cites its specific official sources. The faction bibliography deduplicates URL case. Long contents lists scroll within a bounded space, paragraphs are separated, and all reading chapters start collapsed. Rules and beginner help retain the underlying reading page.

## Editorial distinctions

- Delaque/Nomad origins, Vandoth’s identity, Alpha ancestry and the Stranger’s former service remain theories or rumours.
- The 2024 Spyrer designers’ revision restricts modern hunters to extended House Helmawr. Introductory text agrees and the older concept is explained as publication history.
- The original Hermiatus died; the later figure is a genetic-memory recreation. Malstrain corruption is distinct from straightforward Chaos mutation.
- Legitimate Mercator Pallidus Corpse Harvesters are distinguished from Corpse Grinder cultists.
- Lugrun and Orgo illustrate Ogryn lives in other organisations, not automatic Free Ogryn recruits.
- Aranthian events belong to their published narrative period and do not dictate player campaign outcomes.
- Design influences and interpretive commentary are presented as such; undisclosed answers in commercial books are not invented.

## Research limits

Coverage is accessible official public background, not every page of every commercial book. Paid books are not represented as fully read. No fan wiki, unofficial translation compilation or tutorial contributes new lore. Unrelated Horus Heresy/Age of Sigmar search results and general Kin material were excluded. The old Lost Charter PDF URL returned Bonedry and Broke content and was excluded as a Lost Charter citation. Unreadable PDFs and rules-only FAQs were not lore evidence.

No gameplay catalogue, prices, starting skills, XP rules, account data, roster data or authored descriptions are modified.
